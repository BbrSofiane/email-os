## 1. Corrections to the table

The table is essentially right. These are the corrections and additions:

## 4. Behaviour that looks odd or buggy (all pinned as they are today)

1. **Dead ladder rungs:** the reply-composer and compose-card rungs of row 13 are unreachable, as above.
2. **`s`, Shift+E and Shift+U are always prevented:** `preventDefault` runs even when nothing happens. `s` in fixture mode, which has no star support, is prevented and calls nothing. Shift+E in the Inbox is prevented and calls nothing.
3. **Auto-repeat `e` archives:** the list `e` and the reader `e` have no repeat guard. A repeat `e` event archives the focused row. `s`, `c`, `r`, `h` and `x` do guard repeat, and `z` guards repeat but not Shift.
4. **`z` is always prevented:** a plain `z` is prevented even with nothing to undo.
5. **Escape on body in rule 2 is prevented, but the key does nothing there for modified Escape.**
6. **Row 1 is prevented only for Escape and Tab on body:** Tab from any other element, such as the disabled picker, passes through.
7. **Different ownership by target type:** `window`-dispatched `x`, `j`, `k` and `e` work, but `g`, `ArrowLeft`, `s` and `c` do not, because they need a `Node` target. This only matters for tests and synthetic events, but it is an inconsistency in the handler.
8. **Reader `j` and `k` ignore buttons:** they, and `n`, `p` and `o`, work from a focused toolbar button, while `e`, `s` and `c` do not. The reader navigation keys are not button-blocked.
9. **Shift+`h` on a Reminders row:** the row path uses `key.toLowerCase() === 'h'`, while the plain-`h` path requires `key === 'h'`. So `H` with Shift reaches the row handler as intended. This is consistent, but it relies on the two code paths using different matching.</result>
