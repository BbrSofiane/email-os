## Deviations and open questions

- **Header h1.** The h1 `Reminders` stays visible before the tabs rather than visually hidden. With only the tabs, "Pending / Returned / History" had no name on screen. Its cost is that Reminders and the active tab are both Paper White. Hiding it is a one-line change in `activeTabShown`.
- **Reserved space for row actions.** The row-action cell always takes its width, so the current row doesn't shift. At 968px a very long subject truncates earlier (about 262px in the harness). Collapsing the actions until revealed would trade that for ellipsis jitter.
- **Error banners.** The preparation retry now only appears with its error banner, not as a disabled "Preparing…" button during preparation. "Inspect durable state" is on the error banner.
