## Cognitive-load failures
- **Working memory:** the user has to remember that Drafts and Trash have no chord, that Tab means different things in Inbox and Reminders, and that Reminders tabs are reachable only by Shift+Tab to the header.
- **More than 4 options at once:** the open reschedule picker shows 5 time choices (Later today, Tomorrow, Next week, Someday, Custom), the condition select (2 options) and 2 buttons. A focused Reminders row shows Open, Reschedule and Cancel reminder.
- **Hidden affordances:** the Reminders row actions are invisible on non-current rows, so only row 1 shows them on entry.
- **Layout shift:** the in-flow picker is about 158px tall and pushes the rows down while the user is rescheduling one of them.
- **Chunking:** the Pending tab mixes pending, removing and failed rows.

## Emotional journey
- **Entry:** calm and quick. The ordinary rows are fast, and Back returns to the row you left.
- **Valley:** the failed reminder. It is in coral with no recovery affordance beyond "Cancel reminder", and it carries an ambiguous explanation.

## What is working
1. **Honest state is built into the row.** Phase words keep Pending, Removing…, Failed and Returned separate in color and wording, and a draft is never made unopenable.
2. **The shared machinery holds up.** On Esc, Trash returned focus to the exact row (`conversation-row-conv-05`). Drafts returned to the row I had opened, including after the reader round-trip, and Reminders returned to the Invoice row after the reader.

## Priority issues

**[P1] Reminders keyboard model has no way to change tabs from the list.**
- **Evidence:** `reminders.onTabKeydown` handles arrows only once a tab has focus (`useReminders.ts:95`). There is no list-level key, and Tab from a row moves into Reschedule (`App.vue:2081` applies split-switching only to inbox and saved).
- **Why it matters:** this is the product's keyboard-first core, and Returned and History are effectively mouse-only.
- **Fix:** list-level `[` and `]` (or `1`, `2`, `3`) for Reminders tabs; give Drafts and Trash G chords.
- **Command:** `/impeccable shape`, then `/impeccable harden`.

**[P1] Pending tab counts failed and removing rows.**
- **Evidence:** the render shows "Pending 4" with a Failed row and a Removing… row, and the history bucket holds only cancelled.
- **Why it matters:** the tab count overstates what is pending, which is against the honest-state principle.
- **Fix:** surface a distinct "Needs attention" count or section for failed and removing rows. Give the failed row a Retry or a clear single cause.
