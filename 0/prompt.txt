## The problem
When Gmail definitely rejects one queued mailbox write, the worker ends its pass in sync-error backoff, so the other queued writes (for example the other members of a group archive) stay `queued` until the next retry tick: about a minute, and up to 10 minutes after repeated failures (`MAX_BACKOFF_INTERVAL` in `crates/mail-core/src/gmail/sync.rs`). The browser fake drains them immediately. The owner has decided: **the worker should keep draining past a write that was definitely rejected, and keep backing off when the account or the network is the problem.**

## Where it is
- `crates/mail-core/src/gmail/operations.rs`, `run_one`. It already separates the cases:
  - transient or account-wide errors (`NetworkUnavailable`, `ProviderUnavailable`, `InvalidProviderResponse`, `RateLimited`, `AuthRequired`, `PermissionRequired`, `SecretsUnavailable`) requeue the operation and return `Err`. **Keep this: stop and back off.**
  - the catch-all arm calls `store.fail_operation` and then also returns `Err`. This is the definite, operation-scoped rejection. **This is the one to change**, so the pass continues to the next queued write.
  - `StorageUnavailable` halts. Keep.
  - the account-mismatch branch (`profile.account_id != record.account_id`) is account-wide. Keep it stopping.
- `crates/mail-core/src/gmail/sync.rs` around the `PassAction::Mutations` branch turns `run_one`'s result into `PassOutcome::{Complete, Idle, SyncError, Halt, Cancelled}`.
- `crates/mail-core/tests/contract_scenarios.rs`: `KNOWN_DIVERGENCES` has two KD-1 entries (profiles `gmail` and `synthetic`) for scenario `group-member-failure-does-not-stop-siblings`. The runner requires listed scenarios to fail, so both entries must go once this works.
- `sync.rs` test `synthetic_tick_rearms_queued_writes_after_a_definite_failure` asserts the old behaviour (sibling stays `Queued` until the tick).
