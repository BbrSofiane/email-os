## Monospace fallback
I chose automatic detection over a toggle. It needs no state, no keyboard shortcut and no palette command, so it can't conflict with the other branches editing `ReaderView.vue` and `App.vue`.

A body stays monospace if it has any of these:
- a line starting with `>`, after up to 3 spaces of indent;
- two consecutive lines with 4+ leading spaces, or a tab;
- two or more lines with at least two `|` each.

The text is never altered. The mono variant is capped at 88ch rather than 65ch, so emails hard-wrapped at 72–78 columns aren't soft-wrapped a second time.

The catch is that any reply with a `>` quote will render in mono, and a prose message can't be forced into mono. If you want that, a toggle can be added later.
