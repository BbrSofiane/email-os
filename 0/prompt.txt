## 1. Design-specificity verdict

Authored. Several decisions would not survive being lifted into another product:

- Collapsed message rows reuse the list's 132px sender column. Sender sits at x=90 and snippet at x=234, identical in the list and the reader.
- The date stays on the 24px right inset.
- "Saved locally · not sent" sits next to a Details row that says "Cc: Not cached", with a title explaining why. That is the honest-state principle shown in the UI.
- The composer is an inset card with a Graphite underline per field. It is not a modal and has no Send.
- The hostile-markup fixtures render literally.

What reads as generic is the composer card on its own. It is a rounded raised box with a boxed textarea, which is what any mail client ships. The product's character lives in the reader, not the composer.

## 3. Cognitive load

Failures: 3 of 8, which is moderate load.

- **Single focus: fail in the docked composer.** With a reply open, the thread is squeezed to a sliver. At 1280x800 the scroller drops to 241px with Cc/Subject open. The message being replied to is hidden behind its own composer (its quote sits inside the card).
- **Minimal choices: fail at the header.** Eleven glyphs (the banner note is not one) plus 5 rail stops come before any message. They include two identical clocks and two "Reply" controls (header and per-message).
- **Grouping: fail in Details.** The values start at x=126, 109, 111 and 124 for From, To, Cc and Sent.

Passing: hierarchy, chunking, one thing at a time, working memory, progressive disclosure. The Cc/Subject toggle, caveat toggle and quote toggle are good progressive disclosure.

Decision points with more than 4 visible options: the reader header (about 12 glyphs) and the compact row of reply controls. In the composer itself there are at most 4 controls per state.

## 4. Emotional journey

- **Peak:** J, N, O and Details all behave predictably. The geometry never shifts between conversations. A participant's focus ring is clear. This feels precise.
- **Valley 1:** pressing Enter or clicking Save on an attachment drops focus to the page. The keyboard user loses their place.
- **Valley 2:** R opens a composer that covers half the pane and pushes the contact pane down 33px.
- **End:** Esc returns focus to the message toggle, so the close is clean. In the preview the status line is amber "Saved in preview memory (non-durable) · not sent". That is honest but anxious for a user who just typed. Real state reads Ash.

## 5. Strengths, priority issues, contradicted claims

**Strengths**

1. **Grid parity.** Subject, sender, body, Details and attachments all sit at x=90, the same x as the list sender text. Collapsed rows hit the same x as the list's snippet column (234). The message fill is 46 to 1002 (6px from the 40 and 1008 pane edges), so opening a row never shifts the text edge. J/K lands the same boxes.
2. **Participant focus and Details.** The 2px lavender outline at 1px offset is plainly visible on keyboard focus (verified). The Details glyph is generated content with an empty alt. Cc says "Not cached", never "none".
3. **Safe, honest rendering.** `&lt;script&gt;` and `&lt;img onerror&gt;` render as literal text everywhere. Names are never trusted as markup.

**Priority issues**

- **P1 — Save drops focus to `&lt;body&gt;`.**
  - **What:** In `MessageAttachments.vue`, the Save button is swapped for Saving…/Cancel. Pressing Enter on Save moved `document.activeElement` to BODY (verified). The Retry path has a focus watcher; Save does not.
  - **Why it matters:** Keyboard-first and screen-reader users lose their place on every download.
  - **Fix:** Keep one button mounted (the Retry pattern, `aria-disabled`, label swap), or move focus to the row group and back.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable harden`
- **P1 — Skeleton jumps by 56px, contradicting the claim.**
  - **What:** The row pitch is right (49px each, matching 40.99px + 8px). But in the identity-known case the first skeleton row starts at y=244.3 while the first real message starts at 187.9. The "Loading conversation…" line and the sender/date line push everything down 56px.
  - **Why it matters:** The pane jumps on every J.
  - **Fix:** Fold the status line into the identity line, or reserve its height with the same margins as the loaded meta row.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`
- **P2 — Contact pane shifts while composing a reply.**
  - **What:** "Composing to Ada Fixture / ada@example.test" is inserted at the top (y=55 to 87). The contact name moves from y=87 to 120 and the email from 143 to 176. The new text sits in the header band.
  - **Why it matters:** It breaks the "name on the tab line, email on the first-row line" rule and moves things at the moment you start typing.
  - **Fix:** Keep the pinned lines fixed and put "Composing to…" below the thread list, or drop it.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable layout`
- **P2 — Ragged Details columns.**
  - **What:** `.message-field-label` has no fixed width, so the values start at four different x positions.
  - **Fix:** Give the label column a fixed width (about 3rem) or use a grid.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable polish`
- **P2 — Composer geometry is inconsistent.**
  - **What:** The undocked card is 613px tall (139 to 752) with the footer at y=382 and the rest empty. The reply composer's inner padding is 10px (content at x=101) while the docked new-message card is 16px (x=107). The chooser file list is indented 40px (x=328 against 288 for the text above it).
  - **Fix:** Pin the footer to the bottom or size the card to its content, share one padding value, and zero the chooser list padding.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable layout`
- **P3 — Identical clock glyphs.**
  - **What:** "Remind me on this device" (x=688) and "Reminders" (x=880) are the same clock SVG. Reply also appears twice.
  - **Fix:** A bell or a different mark for the per-conversation reminder.
  - **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## 7. Minor observations and provocative questions

**Minor**

- Hairlines between collapsed messages at 46 to 1002 look like list dividers; the Seams-Only Rule is bent for the reader.
- The Show quoted text ghost button text sits at x=98, 8px inside the 90 text edge. The Save text in the attachment row does the same.
- The Esc hint is bare text beside Save and close. It reads as a label, not a key.
- The synthetic fixtures have no read-state line, so that line was never rendered.
- The reader header shows `— you@fixture.emailos` (account address) in every reader state.
- Signature and quote share one 15px Ash style, so they are not distinguishable from each other.

**Questions**

1. If the thread is the reason to reply, why does the composer get to cover it? Should the composer scroll the latest message into a fixed, visible strip?
2. The product's promise is honest state. Why is the least honest state, a Save that silently moves focus, the one the keyboard user hits most?
3. Is the composer card a decision about design (an inset card with a field underline), or a default that happened to line up with the inset?
