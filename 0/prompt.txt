### Cognitive load checklist failures
- **Primary action not first in Tab order.** In `not_configured` the tabbables are Destinations, Starred, Commands, the 4 rail buttons, then the empty-line `Account` link as the 8th stop.
- **Contradictory instruction.** The same device that says "Add a Google client ID" also offers `Connect Gmail` in the palette and the Account drawer.
- **Status tone lost where status is the whole screen.** Failed, pending and sign-in expired are visually identical in the zero-row view.
- **A dead-looking control.** At 900px with no rows, the rail `Contact` button opens a drawer containing only "Contact" and "Close".

### Emotional journey
- **First launch, not configured.** Calm and clear. The next step is stated, but the main action is a small 16px-tall underlined link.
- **Disconnected.** Good. `Not connected. Connect Gmail` plus a read-only reassurance line.
- **Failure with an empty inbox.** Flat. The tone gives no sense that something is wrong, while the drawer does colour it coral.
- **Disconnect.** Trustworthy: the coral cue, an explicit consequence list, and a safe default focus.

### Strengths
1. **One-fact-one-place logic in the empty line.** The header note is `visually-hidden` but keeps its `role=status` announcement, and the cause appears in words.
2. **The Disconnect confirm.** Coral border and text, `Keep account` focused by default, Esc cancels, and focus returns to the trigger.
3. **The sync-failure palette.** `Sync now` is first and highlighted.
4. **Drawer facts hidden when not real.** The disconnected drawer is just `Connection · Not connected · Connect Gmail`.

### Priority issues
**P2. Failure and pending are indistinguishable in zero-row states.**
- **Evidence:** the empty line colour is `rgb(192,188,182)` for `sync_error_*`, `syncing_first` and `authenticating` alike. The hidden `tone-danger` note carries the colour, but nobody sees it.
- **Why:** this breaks Status-Is-Sacred, because the drawer and the with-rows header colour failure coral.
- **Fix:** give `.list-state` a `data-tone` (danger or warning) taken from the notice and tint only the sentence, not the link.
- **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

**P2. Connect Gmail is offered when the device is not configured.**
- **Evidence:** `actionAvailable('connect')` includes `not_configured` at `src/App.vue:1716`, and `showConnect` includes it at `AccountPanel.vue:99`. In the render, the palette lists `Connect Gmail` first. Running it in the harness flipped the inbox to "No conversations in the cached inbox." That is harness behaviour, but it shows the control is live.
- **Why:** this contradicts DESIGN gap 18, which says no Connect when not configured.
- **Fix:** drop `not_configured` from both and show the next-step line in the drawer.
- **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**P2. At 1030px the squeeze moved from the tabs onto the status.**
- **Evidence:** with two tabs, `Unread only` and a sync failure, the note text clips to 36px of 145 (`Syn…`) and the preview tag shows `Preview · in-…`. In the connected case the read-only link clips to 80px of its 91px (`Review acces`) at 1030, and to 54px at 1000. The code comment says the link never clips.
- **Why:** the most important fact in the header, the failure, is the one cut to three letters.
- **Fix:** let the preview tag shrink below 5.5rem (or hide it first), or have the cause yield before the word "Sync failed".
- **Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

**P3. Duplicate visible Retry and failure when the drawer is open.**
- **Evidence:** the header shows `Sync failed · no network · Retry` and the drawer shows `Sync failed: no network. Retry`.
- **Fix:** hide the header link while the drawer is open, not only the announcement.
- **Command:** `/impeccable distill`

**P3. Empty chrome.**
- **Evidence:** the blank 272px contact column at 1280px, and the rail `Contact` drawer with nothing in it at 900px.
- **Fix:** collapse the pane and remove the rail button when there are no rows.
- **Command:** `/impeccable layout`
