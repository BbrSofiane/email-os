# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Single-account desktop email dogfooding, macOS first. The operator processes
conversations with the keyboard and uses the provider client as fallback.

## Product Purpose

A complete, trustworthy inbox-processing session. Source of strategic decisions:
`docs/adr/0001-single-account-desktop-email-client.md` and
`superhuman-email-browsing-ux-spec.md`; this context does not expand their scope.

## Brand Personality

Fast, predictable, trustworthy (ADR 0001). Compact Superhuman-inspired browsing,
not a claim of verified behavioral parity.

## Anti-references

No AI destinations, fabricated contact enrichment, permanent competing panels,
card stacks, decorative motion, dead controls or optimistic remote-success claims.

## Design Principles

- Focus, bulk selection and message identity are separate.
- Show evidence and coverage, including unknown and retained facts.
- Keep ordinary reading local and non-mutating.
- Reuse established commands, navigation and recovery surfaces.
- Preserve safe literal plain text and local-only drafts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, explicit action labels, visible focus, editable/IME
shortcut protection, reduced-motion alternatives, selection/outcome announcements.
The existing desktop/contact breakpoints remain authoritative; no new mobile UI
or unverified accessibility certification is implied.
